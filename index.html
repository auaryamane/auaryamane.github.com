Welcome to the 11-Year-Old Spelling Contest!

Are you ready to challenge yourself and show off your spelling skills? This contest is designed especially for 11-year-olds who love words and learning new things. Join us for a fun, exciting, and friendly competition!

Contest Rules

  • Participants must be 11 years old at the time of the contest.
  • Contestants will be given words to spell aloud or write down.
  • No electronic devices allowed during the contest.
  • Each round will increase in difficulty.
  • The winner is the last contestant remaining with correct spellings.
  • Have fun and do your best!
